Sources & Further Reading

This page collects the sources used to build this archive. Most of the material currently referenced here comes from community-maintained Spawnism wikis.

These sources document how particular Spawnist communities, branches, or contributors describe the religion. They should therefore be attributed to the source rather than automatically treated as universal agreement among all Spawnists.

Source policy: A community wiki is a source for what that community documents and believes. If a page describes one branch, interpretation, or contributor's idea, this archive keeps that scope instead of presenting it as universal doctrine.
☾ ───── ✦ ───── ☽

Primary Community Sources

TrueSpawnism Wiki

The main community reference used throughout this archive. Its main page describes Spawnism as a spiritual belief system centered on rebirth, second chances, and the Spawn, and also documents different ways followers may understand and practice those ideas.

Read the TrueSpawnism Wiki

The Branches

A branch reference documenting different interpretations within the Spawnist community. The page includes branches with different understandings of the Spawn, including solar, lunar, divine, self-focused, grateful, angelic, and other interpretations.

Read The Branches

Symbols and Interpretations

A reference for Spawnist symbols and the meanings attributed to them within the community source. It discusses the Spawn Sigil and also documents optional beliefs surrounding Spawn Point Angels.

Read Symbols and Interpretations

Holidays and Rituals

A community reference documenting named Spawnist holidays, rituals, and observances. The page explicitly describes these celebrations as optional rather than mandatory.

Its documented examples include Exsomnis, Somnus, Devotee's Week, New Spawn Year, Veilfall, the Day of Returning, and the Quiet Sanctum, among others.

Read Holidays and Rituals

Offerings

A community guide describing personal shrines, altars, worship, and offerings. It emphasizes that shrines can be simple or elaborate and that their design can be personal.

The page also provides examples of objects that its source considers appropriate for symbolic offerings and distinguishes those from things it says should not be offered.

Read Offerings

The Book of Spawnism and Spawnism "Bible"

A community page collecting information about texts associated with Spawnism. It describes the Book of Spawnism as a work containing information about beliefs, holidays, rituals, symbols, and related subjects.

The page also makes clear that the text is unfinished and that Spawnism is not presented there as being tied exclusively to one book.

Read The Book of Spawnism and Spawnism "Bible"

Additional Community Reference

Spawnism Religion Wiki

Another community-maintained Spawnism wiki with its own descriptions of the religion, branches, symbols, history, and practices.

This source is useful for comparison because its descriptions are not necessarily identical to the TrueSpawnism Wiki. Differences between community sources should be preserved rather than silently merged into one supposed universal doctrine.

Read the Spawnism Religion Wiki

How This Archive Uses Sources

General descriptions

Broad descriptions are kept short and are not expanded into specific doctrines unless a source supports the additional information.

Branch-specific information

If a belief belongs to a particular branch, the branch is named. A branch's beliefs are not automatically treated as beliefs held by every Spawnist.

Community wiki information

Claims taken from community wikis are attributed to those wikis. This is especially important for historical claims, religious doctrine, named observances, and disputed interpretations.

Personal interpretation

Personal beliefs and interpretations are labeled as personal. They are not presented as official doctrine simply because they appear in an archive or community discussion.

Conflicting sources

When two Spawnist community sources describe something differently, the difference is preserved. The archive does not automatically choose one account and declare it universally correct.

About Community Wikis

The sources used here are community-maintained websites. Their value is primarily in documenting how participants describe Spawnism, its branches, symbols, practices, and history.

Community-maintained information can change over time. Pages may also contain contributor-specific material, unfinished projects, or interpretations that are not shared throughout the wider community.

Group-Specific Context

Spawnism-related communities and groups should not automatically be treated as interchangeable. If information concerns a particular controversial, harmful, or cult-related group, it belongs in a clearly separated section with its own sources.

Such information should not be used to describe Spawnists generally unless the source specifically supports that scope.
