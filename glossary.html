Words, concepts, and community terms

Glossary

This glossary collects terms used when discussing Spawnism, reincarnation, Spawnist traditions, symbols, practices, and community sources.

Definitions are written for this archive and should not automatically be treated as universal doctrine. When a term has a particular meaning in a branch or community source, that scope is identified.

How to read this glossary: A glossary entry explains how a word is being used here. It does not mean that every Spawnist, branch, or community uses the term in exactly the same way.
☾ ───── ✦ ───── ☽

Core Concepts

Spawnism

A religious belief system centered on the idea of reincarnation or rebirth. The specific beliefs, practices, symbols, and interpretations associated with Spawnism can vary between branches and individuals.

Spawnist

A person who identifies with Spawnism or follows a Spawnist interpretation or tradition.

Individual Spawnists may have different beliefs or practices. Identifying as Spawnist does not by itself establish that a person follows every belief documented by a particular community source.

Reincarnation

The belief or concept that a person or consciousness can be born again in another life after death. Reincarnation is the central concept around which the basic description of Spawnism is organized.

Rebirth

The return to another life or state of existence after a previous life. In this archive, “rebirth” is often used alongside “reincarnation,” although particular sources may give the terms different meanings.

Life Cycle

The broader idea of existence as a cycle rather than a single lifetime. In a Spawnist context, this can refer to the relationship between living, dying, and being reborn.

Branches and Interpretations

Branch

A distinct tradition, interpretation, or community within the broader Spawnist sphere. A belief associated with one branch should be identified as belonging to that branch rather than being automatically presented as universal Spawnist doctrine.

Interpretation

A particular understanding of a Spawnist belief, symbol, practice, or teaching. Different interpretations can exist without every interpretation being shared by every Spawnist.

Personal Interpretation

An individual's own understanding of Spawnism. Personal interpretations should be labeled as personal when documented on this site so that they are not mistaken for established community doctrine.

Community Source

A source created or maintained by members of a Spawnist community, such as a community wiki. Community sources can be useful for documenting terminology, traditions, and how participants describe their beliefs, but their claims should be attributed to the source.

Symbols

Sun Spawn

The first of the two Spawn symbols documented on this site's Symbols page. It is represented by solar imagery and is paired with the Moon Spawn.

Moon Spawn

The second of the two Spawn symbols documented on this site's Symbols page. It is represented by lunar imagery and is paired with the Sun Spawn.

Sun Spawn and Moon Spawn

The paired solar and lunar Spawn symbols. Their exact significance should be understood according to the Spawnist source or interpretation in which they are being used.

Symbol

An image, mark, object, or visual motif used to represent an idea, tradition, person, branch, or religious concept.

Practices

Ritual

A repeated or structured religious practice. Rituals can vary according to individual belief, branch, or community tradition.

Prayer

A form of religious communication, reflection, or devotion. Whether prayer is used, and what form it takes, can differ between individuals and traditions.

Offering

An object, food, creative work, or other item intentionally given as part of a religious or symbolic practice. Offerings should be attributed to the particular tradition or source describing them.

Shrine

A designated space containing objects, images, symbols, or other items associated with religious reflection or personal devotion. A personal shrine does not necessarily represent a universal Spawnist requirement.

Observance

A day, period, event, or practice observed for religious, commemorative, or community purposes. Dates listed on this site should be understood according to their documented source rather than assumed to be universally observed.

Community Observances

Sources and Documentation

Attribution

Identifying where a claim, definition, image, or interpretation came from. This archive uses attribution to distinguish sourced information from personal notes.

Scope

The group, branch, community, source, or individual to which a statement applies. Scope is especially important when a belief is not shared universally.

Primary Community Source

A community-maintained source that directly documents the terminology, beliefs, practices, symbols, or history being discussed. For this archive, the TrueSpawnism Wiki is used as a major community reference.

Universal Claim

A statement presented as applying to all Spawnists. This archive avoids making universal claims unless the available evidence supports that scope.

Source Note

A short explanation identifying the source behind an entry. A useful source note can include the source name, a direct link, and the branch or community being described.

Related Terms

Religion

A system of beliefs, practices, traditions, or interpretations concerning religious or spiritual matters. Spawnism is described on this archive as a religion centered on reincarnation or rebirth.

Doctrine

A formally or informally established teaching or set of teachings. This word should be used carefully when discussing individual or branch-specific Spawnist beliefs.

Tradition

A belief, practice, symbol, observance, or custom passed through or maintained by a particular community or group.

Personal Practice

A religious or reflective activity chosen by an individual rather than necessarily required by a wider tradition.

Rebirth Cycle

A descriptive term for the repeated relationship between lives and rebirth within reincarnation-based belief systems. Its exact interpretation can vary depending on the source.
