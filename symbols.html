Marks and meanings

Symbols

Spawnism uses several symbols and visual motifs. The reference image above shows the Sun Spawn and Moon Spawn symbols together.

Attribution matters: Symbol meanings can belong to a particular Spawnist tradition, branch, or interpretation. They should not automatically be treated as universal beliefs.
☾ ───── ✦ ───── ☽

Sun Spawn

The first symbol is the Sun Spawn. It represents the Sun Spawn within Spawnist symbolism.

The Sun Spawn is associated with the solar side of Spawnist symbolism and provides a counterpart to the Moon Spawn. Its use should be understood within the source and tradition in which the symbol appears.

Moon Spawn

The second symbol is the Moon Spawn. It represents the Moon Spawn within Spawnist symbolism.

The Moon Spawn provides the lunar counterpart to the Sun Spawn. As with other religious symbols, its exact significance can depend on the particular Spawnist interpretation using it.

Sun Spawn and Moon Spawn

The two symbols can therefore be understood as a paired set: Sun Spawn and Moon Spawn. Their solar and lunar imagery creates a visual contrast between the two forms of Spawn represented by the symbols.

This pairing is separate from the decorative moons and stars used throughout this website. The website's celestial decorations are aesthetic choices and should not automatically be interpreted as additional Spawnist religious symbols.

Symbols and branches

Different Spawnist interpretations can place different importance on particular symbols. A symbol may be especially meaningful to one branch, community, or individual without being required elsewhere.

The TrueSpawnism Wiki's branches page documents multiple interpretations within the wider Spawnist community.

Personal symbols

Individual Spawnists may also use personal images, objects, colors, drawings, or other symbols to represent their own relationship with the belief. Personal symbolism should be labeled as personal rather than presented as an official religious symbol.

Sources

The main community reference for Spawnist symbols is the TrueSpawnism Wiki — Symbols and Interpretations. For wider background, see the TrueSpawnism Wiki and this site's Sources & Further Reading page.
