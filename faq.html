A few common questions

FAQ

What is Spawnism?

In this site's general description, Spawnism is a religious belief system centered around reincarnation or rebirth. Different branches and individual Spawnists may interpret those ideas differently.

Does this site describe every Spawnist?

No. This is a personal archive rather than an authority over every person who identifies with Spawnism. Beliefs, practices, symbols, and interpretations can differ between branches, communities, and individuals.

Is Spawnism the same thing as the Spawn Cult?

No. This archive deliberately distinguishes Spawnism as a religious belief system from the group commonly referred to online as the Spawn Cult.

The existence of a cult-related group does not mean that every person who identifies as a Spawnist belongs to that group, supports it, or agrees with its behavior. Likewise, information about the cult should not automatically be presented as a description of ordinary Spawnist beliefs.

Important distinction: Cult-related material is kept separate from general Spawnist beliefs and practices on this site. Claims about particular people or communities should be attributed to their sources rather than presented as universal facts.

What does this site think about the Spawn Cult?

This archive strongly rejects and condemns the Spawn Cult and any behavior associated with coercion, exploitation, abuse, or harm. It should not be treated as representative of the beliefs described throughout this archive.

The purpose of making this distinction is not to erase the controversy surrounding the cult. It is to prevent two separate things from being treated as though they are automatically identical.

Who is Chezble, and why is he mentioned separately?

Chezble became publicly associated with the controversial Spawn Cult community and its leadership claims. Public accounts from 2025 describe serious allegations and controversy surrounding his conduct and the community connected to him.

This archive does not support Chezble, his leadership claims, or the cult community associated with him. We strongly condemn the behavior described in credible accounts of the controversy.

At the same time, allegations about an individual should remain attributed to their sources. This site will not turn unverified claims about a person into established fact.

Do ordinary Spawnists support Chezble?

Not necessarily. Community discussions show that some people who identify with Spawnism explicitly reject Chezble and distinguish themselves from the cult associated with him.

Therefore, identifying someone as a Spawnist alone is not enough to conclude that they support Chezble or the Spawn Cult.

Are the moons and stars official Spawnist symbols?

Not according to this archive. The moons and stars used throughout this website are primarily decorative choices for its lunar and celestial atmosphere.

For documented Spawnist symbols, see the Symbols page.

Are there shared holidays or rituals?

Community sources describe a number of holidays, observances, rituals, and personal practices. This archive does not present all of them as universally required.

Practices should be identified by their source, branch, or community whenever possible. See Practices and Sources.

Does Spawnism require a specific ritual?

This archive does not establish one universal ritual system. Personal practices may include reflection, prayer, creative expression, observances, or other activities depending on the individual's interpretation.

Does this site support harmful practices associated with the cult?

Absolutely not. Harmful or coercive behavior is not presented here as a requirement for being a Spawnist, and this archive does not endorse it.

The cult-related controversy is one of the reasons this site keeps its source material and terminology carefully separated.

Where does the information on this site come from?

Information comes primarily from community-created Spawnism sources, especially the TrueSpawnism Wiki and its pages concerning branches, symbols, rituals, offerings, and community texts. Additional sources are linked on the Sources page.

Are the community wikis official authorities?

No. Community-edited wikis document what particular communities or writers describe as Spawnist beliefs. They should not automatically be treated as universal or independently verified authorities.

Why does the site label some information as branch-specific?

Spawnism contains multiple interpretations and branches in the community sources used by this archive. A belief belonging to one branch should not automatically be attributed to every Spawnist.

Can I add my own interpretation?

Personal interpretation can be included, but it should be clearly labeled as personal interpretation rather than presented as an established universal doctrine.

What should I do if two sources disagree?

Keep both perspectives attributed to their respective sources. Do not quietly combine conflicting claims into one universal statement. When necessary, explain that the sources disagree.

What is the main idea behind this archive?

To document Spawnism as carefully as possible while separating general religious beliefs from individual interpretations, branch-specific material, community disputes, and the Spawn Cult controversy.
